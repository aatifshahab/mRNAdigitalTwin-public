CCTC — Input Parameters

1) Feed mRNA concentration (c_s,in)
   Unit: g/L | Default: 0.5
   Meaning: Bulk mRNA entering the resin bed; drives loading and breakthrough.
   Notes: Also used to set states0_last_value for the bulk phase.

2) Max capacity (q_max)
   Unit: g/L_resin | Default: 2.32
   Meaning: Langmuir capacity; sets the maximum bound concentration in resin.
   Effect: Higher q_max raises bound plateau; influences overall uptake.

3) Affinity (Langmuir K)
   Unit: L/g | Default: 1.0
   Meaning: Langmuir affinity constant; shifts isotherm left/right.
   Effect: Higher K increases binding at a given bulk concentration.

4) Adsorption rate (k_ad)
   Unit: 1/s | Default: 0.1
   Meaning: Kinetic rate toward the isotherm.
   Effect: Larger k_ad accelerates uptake and steepens breakthrough.

5) Intraparticle diffusivity (D_p)
   Unit: m²/s | Default: 1e-10
   Meaning: Diffusion inside particles.
   Effect: Lower D_p slows internal mass transfer; sharper fronts.

6) Film transfer coefficient (k_f)
   Unit: m/s | Default: 1e-5
   Meaning: External mass transfer around beads.
   Effect: Larger k_f reduces boundary-layer limitations.

7) Particle porosity (ε_p)
   Unit: – | Default: 0.35
   Meaning: Void fraction inside beads.
   Effect: Affects internal volumes and accumulation dynamics.

8) Bed void fraction (φ)
   Unit: – | Default: 0.40
   Meaning: Interstitial voids in the packed bed.
   Effect: Enters bulk balance; impacts effective velocities.

9) Resin size-bin fractions (f1, f2, f3)
   Unit: – | Defaults: 0.15, 0.15, 0.15 (sum ≈ 1)
   Meaning: Distribution across resin size bins.
   Effect: Weights each bin’s contribution; adjust for lot variability.

10) Simulation horizon (t_final)
    Unit: s | Default: 500
    Meaning: End time for integration.

11) Time step (Δt)
    Unit: s | Default: 60
    Meaning: Output sample spacing; smaller to resolve short transients.
