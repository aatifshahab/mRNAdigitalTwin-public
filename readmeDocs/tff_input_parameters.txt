Membrane (TFF) — Input Parameters 
Feed flow rate (qF)
Unit: mL/min | Default: 1.0
Meaning: Feed flow through the membrane module.
Effect: Higher qF generally increases shear/flux.

mRNA feed concentration (c0_mRNA)
Unit: mg/mL | Default: 1.0
Meaning: mRNA entering the TFF.
Notes: In chained runs, auto-filled from upstream (IVT μM → mg/mL; CCTC g/L ≡ mg/mL).

Protein feed concentration (c0_protein)
Unit: mg/mL | Default: 0.5
Meaning: Protein entering the TFF.
Effect: Appears in PDE and per-stage diafiltration curves; used for impurity clearance tracking.

NTPs feed concentration (c0_ntps)
Unit: mg/mL | Default: 0.5
Meaning: Nucleotide triphosphates entering the TFF.
Effect: Tracked in PDE/diafiltration; typically targeted for removal during diafiltration.

Desired conversion (X)
Unit: – | Default: 0.9
Meaning: Target fraction used to set the operating flux.

Number of TFF stages (n_stages)
Unit: – (integer) | Default: 3
Meaning: Total stages in the diafiltration train.
Effect: More stages enable deeper diafiltration/removal of low-retained species.

Buffer flow rate (D)
Unit: mL/min | Default: 4
Meaning: Diafiltration buffer feed.
Effect: Higher D accelerates washout of weakly retained species.

Filter type (filterType)
Unit: – | Default: VIBRO
Meaning: Vibration or No vibration
